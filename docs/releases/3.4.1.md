## EchoNyla 3.4.1

Getting past macOS's privacy lock in Files.

- **Open Privacy Settings.** When macOS keeps EchoNyla out of a folder (Desktop, Documents, Downloads, iCloud Drive, a plugged-in drive), Files now says which switch to turn on, like “Desktop Folder”, with a button that opens that page of System Settings, and Try Again.
- **It looks again by itself.** Come back to EchoNyla after turning the switch on and the folder shows, without clicking anything.
