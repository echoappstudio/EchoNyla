## EchoNyla 3.3.1

Previews are quicker and easier.

- **One click previews a file right away.** Click anywhere on a file's row, not just its icon or name, and Quick Look opens at once. Before, every click waited to see whether a second one was coming.
- **A double-click opens the file in its app**, such as Word or Preview, and closes the preview.
- **A faster hover picture.** Rest the pointer on a file for about a third of a second and a larger picture shows above it. The picture starts loading as soon as the pointer arrives, so it's usually ready.
- **Rows light up under the pointer**, with a hint: "Click to preview · Double-click to open". Checkboxes, menus and buttons in the row work as before, and Space still previews the file under the pointer.
