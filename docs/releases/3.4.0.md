## EchoNyla 3.4.0

The biggest update since Apple Intelligence: a new Files area, a preview pane on every page, and clicks that work the same everywhere.

### Files: a Finder that knows your files
- **Browse your Mac** from the new Files page: Home, Desktop, Documents, Downloads, iCloud Drive, Applications, your folders and plugged-in drives. List or icons, Back and Forward, a path bar, sorting by name, kind, size or date, and a list that updates as files arrive.
- **Everyday file work**: drag files into folders (or onto a place, the path bar or the other side), rename (Return), new folder (⇧⌘N), copy and paste, Open With, Show in Finder, and Move to Trash (⌘⌫). Every move, rename and trip to the Trash can be undone (⌘Z).
- **Where it belongs.** In folders like Downloads and Desktop, each file can show the folder it belongs in, with a Move button.
- **Clearer names in grey.** A sure name from Apple Intelligence shows beside a file like `IMG_4821.jpg`. Click it to use it.
- **Sort with the keyboard.** Your nine most-used folders are numbered at the top. Press ↓ to go through the files and 1 to 9 to send one to that folder; 0 skips it.
- **Group by what files are**: receipts, bank statements, taxes, screenshots, pictures, recordings, installers and more. Also by kind or by date.
- **Clutter flags**: exact copies (checked byte for byte), installers for apps you already have, files over 500 MB and files nobody opened in a year. **Tidy This Folder** opens Clean Up for the folder, where all of it waits for you to approve or skip.
- **Search in plain words**, inside the folder you're in. Type to narrow the list by name; press Return to search, like “tax PDFs from last spring”.
- **Two folders side by side**, so you can drag between them. Tab switches sides.
- **Folder sizes**, worked out in the background, and hovering over a folder says what's inside it.

### The same clicks everywhere
- **One click** selects a file and shows it in the new **preview pane** on the right, on every page. The pane can be hidden with the button at the top right.
- **Space or a firm press** on the trackpad opens the full Quick Look preview.
- **Double-click** opens the file (or goes into a folder in Files).
- **Resting the pointer** for half a second shows a small card: the picture, name, kind, size, date, what it is and where it belongs.

### The preview pane
- A big picture of the file, what Apple Intelligence says it is, the people, places and companies in it, the words spoken in the first minute of a recording or video, and its details (created, modified, last opened, pages, where it was downloaded from).
- Suggestions with Rename and Move buttons.
- **History**: what EchoNyla did to the file, like “Renamed from IMG_4821.jpg” and “Moved here from Downloads”, with Undo.

### Names and copies
- **Every new name is listed, old → new**, in Deep Reorganize and Clean Up, including the ones Apple Intelligence isn't sure about, each with its checkbox.
- **Files Deep Reorganize moved by itself keep their suggested name waiting** for you, instead of losing it.
- **Exact copies go to the Trash by themselves in Deep Reorganize.** Only files whose every byte matches another file; the one with the clearest name (or the oldest) stays. It's on by default and can be turned off (in Deep Reorganize or Settings). Undo All brings the copies back. Nothing else is ever put in the Trash without you.
